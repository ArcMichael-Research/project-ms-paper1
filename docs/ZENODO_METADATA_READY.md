**Description**  
Project M/S Paper 1 presents a preliminary applied cybersecurity study that separates AI decision recommendations from downstream governance outcomes. Three synthetic component experiments establish a typed-decision recording path, examine twelve vulnerability-classification cases, and evaluate five governance-boundary cases. In the final experiment, observed proposals and predefined PATCH counterfactuals are replayed across three governance conditions. The release includes the manuscript, preserved experiment evidence, recovered historical runners where available, an offline evidence dashboard, reproducibility notes, and a proposed Phase 2 architecture. The study makes narrow claims and does not establish general model robustness, production safety, or broad framework novelty.
