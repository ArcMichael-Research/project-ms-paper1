# Author contribution and AI assistance

Peter P Chavez conceived and directed Project M/S, defined its research objectives, experimental scope, governance architecture, and scenarios, directed the laboratory experiments, and made all final decisions on interpretation and the manuscript.

OpenAI ChatGPT was used in a supporting role: as a tutor and sounding board for learning methods and critiquing ideas; to generate and debug portions of the code, including the dashboard; and to draft and refine portions of the manuscript text. The author reviewed, tested, and edited all AI-assisted code and text. AI-generated suggestions and assessments were not treated as independent validation. The author takes full responsibility for the reported methods, code, evidence, references, interpretations, and conclusions. AI tools are not authors.
